# Resume writing guide research basis

This material informs the editorial rules in [`skills/resume-toolkit/writing-guide.md`](../skills/resume-toolkit/writing-guide.md). Resume conventions here are guidance, not guarantees of screening outcomes; several items are expert or vendor commentary rather than controlled research. Direct fetching of the Reddit wiki returned 403 during research, so its indexed guidance is cross-checked against university career offices and engineering-specific sources.

- [r/EngineeringResumes wiki](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/wiki/index/), [software guide](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/wiki/software/), and [checklist](https://www.reddit.com/r/EngineeringResumes/wiki/checklist/) — accomplishment-focused bullets, XYZ/STAR/CAR, relevance ordering, concise writing, and concrete technical context.
- [UT Austin Career Services: Strong Bullets for Technical Resumes](https://careerservices.cns.utexas.edu/resources/resumes/strong-bullets-technical-resumes) — explain what was built, why it mattered, and how; quantify users, teams, usage, and work scope where available.
- [Yale Office of Career Strategy: Writing Impactful Resume Bullets](https://ocs.yale.edu/resources/writing-impactful-resume-bullets/) — action/project/result and XYZ structures; specific personal contribution, measurable impact, and context.
- [SHRM: How to Spot AI-Generated Lies on a Resume](https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/technology/how-to-spot-ai-generated-lies-on-a-resume) (2024) — recruiters interviewed describe probing claims and preferring genuine, explainable experience over embellished wording.
- [Forbes Coaches Council: How Recruiters Can Tell You Used AI On Your Resume—and Why It Matters](https://www.forbes.com/councils/forbescoachescouncil/2025/08/07/how-recruiters-can-tell-you-used-ai-on-your-resume-and-why-it-matters/) (2025) — recruiter observations on generic buzzwords, copy-pasted content, poor job customization, and missing candidate-specific detail. Expert commentary, not a controlled study.
- [Jobscan: Can ATS Detect AI Resumes?](https://www.jobscan.co/blog/can-ats-detect-ai-resume/) (2026) — distinguishes job matching from authorship detection and identifies generic, unedited content as a human-review risk. Vendor article, not independent validation.
